## 🔴 新增反向差评：开锁方式/速度优先级 > 授权管理

以下差评来自近年来智能门锁社区讨论（Reddit r/smarthome, Slickdeals），主题是"用户对智能门锁的期望被过度复杂化"：

| # | 来源 | 差评原文摘要 | 严重度 |
|---|------|------------|--------|
| P31 | Reddit | "Bought a $300 smart lock. I use exactly two features: fingerprint and auto-lock. Everything else is bloat I paid for and never touched." — u/SmartHomeMinimalist | 高 |
| P32 | Reddit | "The app keeps adding 'smart features' I didn't ask for. I just want a lock that opens in under 0.5 seconds, not a 'permission lifecycle management dashboard'." — u/LockItSimple | 高 |
| P33 | Slickdeals | "Returned my eufy after a week. The fingerprint reader was slower than my $20 keypad lock. All the 'AI features' don't matter if the basic unlock is laggy." — deal_hunter_2025 | 高 |
| P34 | Amazon | "I manage 3 rental units and honestly? A lockbox with a physical key is more reliable than any smart lock I've tried. Tenants never complain about a key." — verified_landlord (C220) | 中 |
| P35 | Reddit | "Every smart lock company is competing on 'who has more features.' I want the opposite: who can make the simplest, fastest, most reliable lock that does ONE thing perfectly." — u/LockLuddite | 高 |

**反向聚类洞察**：存在一个被忽视的用户群体——他们不想要"权限管理"或"AI智能"，只想要**更快、更简单、更可靠**的基础开锁体验。这部分需求在目前的知识源中被系统性低估。
